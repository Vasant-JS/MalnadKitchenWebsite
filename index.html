About Us

Pioneers in wholesome food service from Shivamogga.

Malnad Kitchen is a pioneer in offering a wide variety of food services from Shivamogga. Founded by Mr. Krishnamurthy and Mrs. Mamata Krishnamurthy, the company started more than 20 years ago with a vision of serving mankind through wholesome food.

Today, we serve industries, schools, institutions, weddings and events with high-quality vegetarian cuisine. Our philosophy is built on Taste, Quality, Health and Service.

We specialize in authentic Malnad cuisine while also offering other vegetarian cuisines. We also manufacture authentic masala powders and instant mixes that are popular in India and exported to several countries.
